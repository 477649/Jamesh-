Advanced Smart Money Stock Screener

Single-file HTML, CSS, and JavaScript dashboard for floor sheet, share price, and optional symbol-sector-source mapping. Built for GitHub Pages. Includes buy screener, sell screener, sector flow, broker intelligence, risk monitor, symbol deep dive, export tools, and interactive charts.

Required files
Upload floor_sheet.csv and share_price.csv. Optional: symbol_mapping.csv with symbol, sector, source. You can also click Load Demo and test the full dashboard instantly.
Accepted floor sheet aliases: date, symbol, buyer_broker, seller_broker, quantity, rate or price, amount or turnover. Accepted share price aliases: date, symbol, open, high, low, close, volume. Mapping aliases: symbol, sector, source.
